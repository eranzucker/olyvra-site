How it counts

Every calendar day you are in Colombia counts, including the days you arrive and leave. Days are added up over any 365 consecutive days, whether or not they are continuous. Reaching 184 days in any such window meets the day test, and you may be treated as tax resident; if the window spans two years, residency applies from the second year.

The day count is not the only test. Colombian nationals can be tax resident without the days, for example when their spouse or children live in Colombia or most of their income or assets are there. Your visa or tourist permit makes no difference to the count.

This is a day count, not a residency determination; confirm your status with DIAN or a qualified tax adviser.

Questions

Is it 183 days per calendar year?

No. It is any 365 consecutive days, so a stay across New Year can still add up to more than 183. If that window spans two years, residency applies from the second year.

Do arrival and departure days count?

Yes. DIAN counts every calendar day in Colombia, including the days you enter and leave.

What about the 90 and 180 days on my tourist permit?

Those are Migración Colombia's visitor limits: up to 90 days per entry and 180 per calendar year. They are separate from the tax count, and the app tracks them too.
